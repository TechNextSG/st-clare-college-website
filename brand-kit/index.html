Logo: the official seal

The seal is the only logo. It is used exactly as supplied by the college, in full colour, never redrawn, recoloured or cropped.

Clear space

Keep a margin of one quarter of the seal's width on every side. Nothing (text, edges, other logos) enters this space.

Minimum size

Print: 18 mm wide. Screen: 48 px wide (24 px only for browser icons). Below that the ring lettering is unreadable.

Graphic elements

The pieces that make a St. Clare layout recognisable. Combine two or three; never all at once.

Photography

Real St. Clare people and places: the campus, the Virtual Lab, classrooms, graduation. Warm, bright and candid.

Do

  • Use real photos of St. Clare students, staff and campus.
  • Put text over photos only on a maroon scrim (dark at the text side).
  • Leave room on the left or bottom for headlines.
  • Keep skin tones natural; no heavy filters.

Don't

  • Use stock photos of other schools or foreign campuses.
  • Use cartoon or clip-art people.
  • Put white text straight on bright photos.
  • Use photos with old dated offers visible as if they were current.

Boilerplate (copy this)

St. Clare College of Caloocan is the School of Life Skills and a leading institution for professional development. Founded on 31 August 1985 in Caloocan City, Metro Manila, it educates learners from Nursery to College, on campus or fully online through St. Clare Online, with e-boards in all 95 classrooms and a Virtual Lab for mixed reality.

Vision

To become the leading institution for professional development and the first and foremost provider of quality, affordable and sustainable development education.

Mission

To provide quality and excellent global education for the total development of a responsible, innovative, skillful and professional global workforce whose values and dignity are beyond par.

Approved facts

  • Founded 31 August 1985, Caloocan City
  • Levels Nursery to Grade 10, Senior High School, College
