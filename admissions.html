Enrollment procedure

Proceed in order; each step happens at a specific place on campus.

  1. Pay the matriculation fee

    Where: Cashier

    • Proceed to the cashier and pay the matriculation fee (designated cashier only).
    • Get and keep your OFFICIAL RECEIPT.

  2. Fill up the registration form

    Where: Lobby

    • Go to the LOBBY and present your Official Receipt to the Enrollment Officer.
    • Fill up the REGISTRATION FORM.
    • Check that your SUBJECTS are listed on the registration form.

  3. Register online for your schedule

    Where: Library, 3rd floor

    • Proceed to the LIBRARY on the 3rd floor for ENLISTMENT at stclareonline.com/ims.
    • Your section and schedule are posted on the website and sent by email by your Program Head.

What happens next: your Program Head posts your section and schedule on the website and emails them to you. Call (02) 8232-4483 to confirm this school year's enrollment dates and fees.

Frequently asked questions

Can I enroll online?
Yes. Online enrollment is through the IMS: St. Clare IMS. You can also enroll on campus in three steps.
Can I study fully online?
Yes. St. Clare College offers two modalities: full online and blended (online plus on-campus days).
How do I get my class schedule?
After paying the matriculation fee and filling up your registration form, go to the Library on the 3rd floor for enlistment. Schedules and sectioning are posted in the LMS and emailed by your Program Head.
When does enrollment open, and how much are the fees?
Call the admissions office at (02) 8232-4483 or email stclarecollege.ph@gmail.com to confirm this school year's enrollment dates and fees. Fee slides on this page are from earlier school years.
Are there scholarships?
Ask admissions for this school year's offers. Submit a certificate of indigency for scholarship consideration. UniFAST-TES grantees verify their status and schedule on the PMS.
How do I request my Transcript of Records or diploma?
Only through the Official Document Request Portal. Do not transact with fixers.
