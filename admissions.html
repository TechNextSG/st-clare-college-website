Enrollment procedure

Three steps to St. Clare

Bring your requirements in a long brown envelope and follow these steps on campus.

01

Pay the matriculation fee

  • Proceed to the cashier and pay the matriculation fee (designated cashier only).
  • Get and keep your OFFICIAL RECEIPT.
Cashier
02

Fill up the registration form

  • Go to the LOBBY and present your Official Receipt to the Enrollment Officer.
  • Fill up the REGISTRATION FORM.
  • Check that your SUBJECTS are listed on the registration form.
Lobby
03

Register online for your schedule

  • Proceed to the LIBRARY on the 3rd floor for ENLISTMENT at stclareonline.com/ims.
  • Your section and schedule are posted on the website and sent by email by your Program Head.
Library, 3rd floor
Admission requirements

What to bring

Choose your level to see the checklist.

CFCollege freshmen

  1. Senior High School graduate of a public or private school
  2. SHS diploma or certificate of completion
  3. Graduating SHS students: latest grading report card as reflected in the original F138 or report card
  4. PSA or local civil registry birth certificate
  5. 2x2 ID photos, recent (2 pcs.)
  6. Certificate of indigency (optional, for scholarship)
  7. Photocopies of all the documents above
  8. 1 long brown envelope

TRCollege transferees

  1. PSA or local civil registry birth certificate
  2. 2x2 ID photos, recent (2 pcs.)
  3. Transcript of records
  4. Certificate of honorable dismissal
  5. Course description for evaluation
  6. Certificate of indigency (optional, for scholarship)
  7. Photocopies of all the documents above
  8. 1 long brown envelope

BEPre-school to Grade 10

  1. Photocopy of PSA birth certificate
  2. 2 pcs. 2x2 photo
  3. Form 138 or report card (new / transferee)
  4. Form 137 (new / transferee)
  5. Good moral certificate
  6. Long brown envelope

SHSenior High School

  1. Photocopy of PSA birth certificate
  2. Photocopy of Grade 10 diploma
  3. Form 138 or report card
  4. 2 pcs. 2x2 photo
  5. Long brown envelope
  6. Secondary requirements: Form 137 and good moral certificate
  7. Voucher certificate (for private school completers only)

All documents are considered property of the school once submitted as a requirement for admission.

Questions

Good to know

Can I study fully online?
Yes. St. Clare College offers two modalities: full online and blended (online plus on-campus days). Choose the one that fits you when you enroll.
How do I get my class schedule?
After paying the matriculation fee and filling up your registration form, register online for enlistment at the Library on the 3rd floor (stclareonline.com/ims). Your section and schedule are posted on the website and sent by email by your Program Head.
Are there scholarships?
Ask the admissions office for this school year's offers. A certificate of indigency can be submitted with your requirements for scholarship consideration, and UniFAST-TES grantees can verify their status and disbursement schedule on the PMS.
How do I request my Transcript of Records or diploma?
Only through the Official Document Request Portal on the Personal Management System (PMS). Do not transact with fixers or unauthorized persons.
When is the admissions office open?
9:00 AM to 5:00 PM. Call (02) 8232-4483 or email stclarecollege.ph@gmail.com.
