Cashier1

Pay the matriculation fee

  • Proceed to the cashier and pay the matriculation fee (designated cashier only).
  • Get and keep your OFFICIAL RECEIPT.
Lobby2

Fill up the registration form

  • Go to the LOBBY and present your Official Receipt to the Enrollment Officer.
  • Fill up the REGISTRATION FORM.
  • Check that your SUBJECTS are listed on the registration form.
Library, 3rd floor3

Register online for your schedule

  • Proceed to the LIBRARY on the 3rd floor for ENLISTMENT at stclareonline.com/ims.
  • Your section and schedule are posted on the website and sent by email by your Program Head.

What happens next

Your Program Head posts your section and schedule on the website and emails them to you. Office hours are 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM; call ahead to confirm this school year's enrollment dates and fees.

Good to know

Can I study fully online?
Yes. St. Clare College offers two modalities: full online and blended (online plus on-campus days). Choose one when you enroll.
How do I get my class schedule?
After paying the matriculation fee and filling up your registration form, go to the Library on the 3rd floor for enlistment at stclareonline.com/ims. Your section and schedule are posted on the website and emailed by your Program Head.
When does enrollment open, and how much are the fees?
Call the admissions office at (02) 8232-4483 or email stclarecollege.ph@gmail.com to confirm this school year's enrollment dates and fees before your visit.
Are there scholarships?
Ask the admissions office for this school year's offers. Submit a certificate of indigency with your requirements for scholarship consideration. UniFAST-TES grantees can verify their status and disbursement schedule on the PMS.
How do I request my Transcript of Records or diploma?
Only through the Official Document Request Portal on the Personal Management System (PMS). Do not transact with fixers.
