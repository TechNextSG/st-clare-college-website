Eight degree programmes

BSCSCollege of Computing

Bachelor of Science in Computer Science

Proficiency in designing, writing and developing computer programs and computer networks: algorithms, software applications, data processing, web development, programming and computer architecture. Lectures build the theory; the key learning happens in the computer laboratories, working hands-on with software, programming tools and real hardware.

ProgrammingWeb developmentNetworksComputer architecture
BSHMHospitality & Tourism

Bachelor of Science in Hospitality Management

Competencies to run operations and management functions across the hospitality industry. Covers food production (culinary), accommodation, food and beverage service, tourism planning and product development, events planning, and travel and tour operations.

CulinaryAccommodationF&B serviceEvents
BSTMHospitality & Tourism

Bachelor of Science in Tourism Management

Operational and management competencies for hospitality, tourism and their emerging sectors. Food production, accommodation, food and beverage service, tourism planning and product development, events planning, transportation services, and travel and tour operations.

Tourism planningTravel & toursTransport servicesEvents
BSBABusiness

Bachelor of Science in Business Administration

Knowledge, skills and attitude to provide quality service in business and industry. Students practise the basic functions of management (planning, organizing, leading and controlling) through theory classes, practicum exercises and experiential learning.

ManagementPracticumExperiential learning
BEEdTeacher Education

TeachingElementary level

AB PolSciSocial Sciences

Bachelor of Arts in Political Science

Prepares you for a professional career in government service, or for careers dealing with public issues and problems. Core competencies are expressed as observable behaviours, and the curriculum exposes students to a variety of programme work and activities for a broad understanding of the field.

GovernmentPublic issuesPolicy
