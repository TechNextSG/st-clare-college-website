# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML/CSS/JS generated by `build.py`, hosted on GitHub Pages. No framework; CDN libraries allowed.

## Users

Parents and prospective students in Caloocan / Metro Manila choosing a school (Nursery to College) and deciding to enroll. Current Clareans (students, faculty, alumni) use the portal links as a secondary job.

## Product Purpose

St. Clare College of Caloocan's website: make families choose St. Clare and start enrollment (online via IMS or on campus), and route current Clareans to the St. Clare Online systems (LMS, CMS, IMS, PMS, VMS).

## Positioning

"The School of Life Skills" and "the leading edge in online education system": one Caloocan campus (since 1985) plus its own St. Clare Online platform (since 2020), e-boards in all 95 classrooms, a VR/AR Virtual Lab, full online or blended modality, Nursery to College under one roof.

## Operating Context

Families compare schools on phones; enrollment happens in three on-campus steps (cashier, lobby registration, 3rd-floor library enlistment via IMS). Requirements are paper documents in a long brown envelope. Office hours 9:00 AM - 5:00 PM.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Levels: Basic Education (Nursery-Grade 10), Senior High (ABM, HUMSS, Arts & Design, TVL-ICT, TVL-HE), Tertiary (BSCS, BSHM, BSTM, BSBA, BEEd, BSEd-Eng, BSEd-Math, AB PolSci).
- Portals and downloadable forms live on stclareonline.com; this site links out.
- Offers on the source site are dated (SHS Alumni VIP = Class of 2024, SY 2024-2025); never present dated offers as current.

## Brand Commitments

- Real identity assets: the St. Clare College of Caloocan seal and the red SCO Online logo. User pinned nothing else (palette, type and style are open for redesign, 19 Oct 2026).
- Taglines from the school: "The School of Life Skills", "the leading edge in online education system", "Transforming Education, Building Our Future", Professional Mace "The Eternal Flame of Professionalism".

## Evidence on Hand

Photos in `img/`: campus building, graduates (commencement), Virtual Lab (Predator stations), VR student, Basic Ed uniforms. Facts: founded 31 Aug 1985, 38th commencement June 2024, 547,806 views of the 2020 start-up module, e-boards in 95 classrooms. No testimonials, no tuition figures, no rankings: do not invent them.

## Product Principles

1. Enrollment is the primary action on every page.
2. Every fact traces to stclareonline.com.
3. Premium and specific to St. Clare, never a generic school template.
4. Motion carries the story (rich, cinematic) but content stays readable and reduced-motion safe.
