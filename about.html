Milestones

Our story

Four decades of firsts for Caloocan.

1985

St. Clare College is founded

Founded on 31 August 1985 in Caloocan City, Metro Manila, as a school of life skills.

2020

St. Clare Online keeps learning going

During the pandemic the college moved classes to its own online platform so no student got left behind. Applications for UniFAST-TES opened for A.Y. 2020-2021.

2021

Virtual Lab and the 36th anniversary

The Virtual Lab (VR + AR = MR) opened, Teacher-DJs went live on Zoom daily, and the college marked its 36th founding anniversary.

2023

The Professional Mace

On 23 September 2023 St. Clare College unveiled its Professional Mace, the eternal flame of professionalism.

2024

38th Commencement + SHS Alumni VIP

The Stars of 2024 graduated at the 38th Commencement Exercises, and SHS alumni received the President's Scholarship with zero down payment.
