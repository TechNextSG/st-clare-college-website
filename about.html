Transforming education, building our future

St. Clare College of Caloocan is the School of Life Skills and a leading institution for professional development, serving Caloocan since 1985.

Vision and mission

Quality, affordable and sustainable education for the total development of every Clarean.

Vision

To become the leading institution for professional development and the first and foremost provider of quality, affordable and sustainable development education.

Mission

To provide quality and excellent global education for the total development of a responsible, innovative, skillful and professional global workforce whose values and dignity are beyond par.

Milestones

Four decades of firsts for Caloocan.

  1. 1985

    St. Clare College is founded

    Founded on 31 August 1985 in Caloocan City, Metro Manila, as a school of life skills.

  2. 2020

    St. Clare Online keeps learning going

    During the pandemic the college moved classes to its own online platform so no student got left behind. UniFAST-TES applications opened for A.Y. 2020-2021.

  3. 2021

    The Virtual Lab and the 36th anniversary

    The Virtual Lab (VR + AR = MR) opened, Teacher-DJs went live on Zoom Monday to Saturday, and the college marked its 36th founding anniversary.

  4. 2023

    The Professional Mace

    On 23 September 2023 St. Clare College introduced its Professional Mace, “the eternal flame of professionalism” and “a beacon of professionalism and culture of excellence.”

  5. 2024

    The Stars of 2024

    The 38th Commencement Exercises, and the President's Scholarship with zero down payment for the SHS Class of 2024.

No one gets left behind

When colleges around the world moved to online and open distance learning, St. Clare's own platform made sure its students could continue and finish their education.
