Our story

Four decades of firsts for Caloocan.

1985

St. Clare College is founded

Founded on 31 August 1985 in Caloocan City, Metro Manila, as a school of life skills.

2020

St. Clare Online keeps learning going

During the pandemic the college moved classes to its own online platform so no student got left behind. UniFAST-TES applications opened for A.Y. 2020-2021.

2021

The Virtual Lab and the 36th anniversary

The Virtual Lab (VR + AR = MR) opened, Teacher-DJs went live on Zoom Monday to Saturday, and the college marked its 36th founding anniversary.

2023

The Professional Mace

On 23 September 2023 St. Clare College introduced its Professional Mace, the eternal flame of professionalism.

2024

The Stars of 2024

The 38th Commencement Exercises, and the President's Scholarship with zero down payment for the SHS Class of 2024.
